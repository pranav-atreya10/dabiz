Scope: the whole site. Home page is Persuade; sign-in, app, documents, usage and admin are Operate; privacy is Read.
Audience: product people and engineering leads at large companies (first: a Microsoft team). Action: type an idea and start.
Constraints: no fabricated proof; names of tools the product really exports to may appear. Light/dark follows the device.
seed: none (direction pinned by the founder: design/references/06-seal-blue-navy-crimson-pink.webp)

## Direction contract

THESIS: A calm, icy "visionary intelligence" world where the idea goes in on the left and passes through fluted glass on the right. Refuses the SaaS split hero with a screenshot and a badge row of fake customer logos.

OWN-WORLD: Seal Blue #DBF2FC ground rising to deeper blue, Navy #072D59 ink and dark-mode ground, Crimson Pink #FD6E88 only for the one action per screen and selection. Hanken Grotesk light display with an italic second line, regular body. Pill controls. Signature: a deep blue sphere with a pink rim seen through vertical fluted-glass ribs.

STORY: An idea becomes questions, then requirements, then a spec, then work items and a coding-tool kit.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Big light headline with italic second line, one sentence, a working idea field with a pink Start pill, a "sends to" row of real tool names, and the glass-and-sphere artwork bleeding off the right edge with a live question card floating over it.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: the glass ribs refract the sphere and shift with the pointer; the question card cycles through real example questions.

RISK: glass can read as decoration; it must stay one specific object, not scattered blur.
