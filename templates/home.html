Common questions.

Who is it for?

Anyone who turns ideas into work for engineers: product managers, founders, team leads and architects. If you've ever had to write requirements from a one-line idea, it's for you.

Do I need to be technical?

No. The first questions are about how the product should work, not how it's built. The technical spec has its own questions, and you can skip any of them: the spec lists what it assumed.

Can I change what the AI writes?

Yes. Every document and spec has an Edit tab, and your changes are saved. You can also ask for more questions before the document is written.

Does it work with Azure DevOps?

Yes. Once your admin connects your organization's project, one click creates the epics and user stories, each story linked under its epic.

Can I use it with AI coding tools?

Yes. Download a starter folder for Codex, Claude Code, Cursor or GitHub Copilot, or copy everything as one prompt for any other tool.

Where does my data go?

Your idea and answers are sent to OpenAI to write the questions and documents. Documents are saved in your organization's private space, and other organizations can't see them. Read the privacy page for details.

How do I get access?

Each organization signs in with its own code. If you don't have one yet, ask the person who invited you to try Requirements Studio.
