Privacy

What happens to the information you put into Requirements Studio, in plain words.

What we store

  • Your organization's name, and a scrambled (hashed) version of its sign-in code. We can't read the code itself.
  • Your documents: the idea you typed, your answers to the questions, and the document that was written.
  • Feedback you give on a document (thumbs up or down, and any comment).
  • Usage counts: how many AI tokens each request used, and when. Not the text itself.
  • Azure DevOps settings, if your admin connected them. The access token is stored encrypted.

Who it's shared with

  • OpenAI: your idea, answers and document are sent to OpenAI's API to write questions and documents. See OpenAI's policies for how they handle API data.
  • Azure DevOps: only when you press "Send to Azure DevOps", the document is sent to your organization's own Azure DevOps project.
  • Our hosting and database providers (Vercel and the database host) store the data above so the app can work.

We don't sell your data or use it for ads.

Who can see it

  • Anyone with your organization's code can see your organization's documents. Other organizations can't.
  • The site admin can see feedback and the documents it was left on, to improve the product.

Please don't include

Passwords, personal details about people (like health or financial information), or anything your company isn't allowed to share with outside services.

Deleting your data

  • You can delete any document yourself from My documents.
  • To delete everything for your organization, ask your admin. Deleting an organization deletes its documents and settings. Its usage counts are kept without its name.

Last updated October 2026. This is a plain-language summary for an early version of the product, not a legal agreement.
